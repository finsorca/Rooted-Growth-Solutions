Who We Are

A Joint Venture Built on Complementary Expertise

Green Cultivars Solutions is a specialised joint venture that brings together agribusiness and financial management expertise alongside geospatial analysis capability. Together, we deliver what no single discipline can offer alone: a complete, integrated picture of a farm's spatial assets and its financial performance.

Our work addresses a persistent gap in smallholder support across South Africa. While NGOs, government institutions, and private organisations provide valuable assistance, that support is rarely integrated. We connect the dots between where a farmer works and how that translates into sustainable profit.

The Challenge We Address

A Persistent Gap in Smallholder Support

Challenge 01

No Structured Financial Systems

Most smallholder farmers operate without access to farm-level financial tools. Enterprise budgets, cash flow plans, and cost structures that reveal true profitability remain out of reach for the majority.

Challenge 02

Limited Farm-Level Data

Decisions are made without reliable spatial or production data. Farmers cannot identify underperforming zones or make informed choices about where to invest their time, labour, and inputs for the best return.

Challenge 03

Disconnected Planning and Finance

Even where support exists, spatial analysis and financial management are delivered in isolation. Plans look sound on paper but fail to account for the financial reality of each production zone on the farm.

Challenge 04

Poor Resource Allocation

Without knowing which land units are productive and at what cost, farmers spread inputs too thin or invest where returns are lowest. Margins erode season after season and enterprise viability is undermined.

How We Work

A Practical, Step-by-Step Methodology

01

Engage & Assess

We begin with each farmer individually, understanding their land, enterprise mix, challenges, and goals. This baseline assessment forms the foundation for everything that follows in the programme.

02

Map the Farm

Using QGIS, we produce accurate farm maps covering boundaries, production zones, infrastructure, and current land utilisation, giving farmers a clear and reliable spatial picture of their assets.

03

Build the Numbers

Enterprise budgets, cost structures, and cash flow projections are developed for each farmer, creating a financial baseline that is directly connected to their land and production decisions.

04

Integrate & Plan

Spatial and financial data are brought together into a practical farm business plan that aligns land use with the enterprises that are most financially viable for that specific farm context.

05

Monitor & Improve

Reporting tools and regular check-ins keep farmers on track and enable programme partners to demonstrate measurable, evidence-based impact to their stakeholders and funders.

Phase 1 Programme

The Pilot Initiative

Our first phase focuses on a carefully selected group of 10 to 20 smallholder farmers across KwaZulu-Natal. This pilot is designed to test, refine, and prove the integrated model in a real agricultural context before scaling it through programme partnerships.
